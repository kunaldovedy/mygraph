A jet engine compressor rings like a tuning fork when you drop a ball bearing into it. A practice works the same way: something goes in at the centre, and the sound comes out at the edge. This graph reads outward through eight stages, from what must stay true, through what gets made, to the people on the other end. The dashed lines come back.
