## Free

You owe nothing but the notice.

- **Individuals.** Mapping your own practice, study, hobby projects,
  experiments, research, teaching.
- **Small companies.** Any company with fewer than 100 people, counting
  employees and contractors, **and** under 1,000,000 USD revenue in the
  previous tax year. The licence indexes that figure to United States
  inflation from 2019, so it rises over time.
- **Consultants and freelancers** working under those thresholds, including
  on paid client work.

If you are under both limits, nothing here applies to you beyond keeping the
notice. Take it and go.

## Needs a paid licence

A company over either threshold, 100 people or 1,000,000 USD revenue, needs a
licence to use it for the company's benefit. That covers internal use,
client work, embedding it in a product or service, and using it in material
that sells something.

If you are not sure which side of the line you are on, ask. A short email is
cheaper than a guess, and if you are close to the threshold the answer is
usually yes, carry on.
